U.S. Court of Appeals, 2nd Circuit (2023): A commercial truck driver was fired after failing a drug test, which he blamed on a CBD product the companies marketed as THC-free. He sued under civil RICO for his lost job. The district court dismissed the RICO claim because lost earnings flowing from a personal injury are not an injury to 'business or property.' The Second Circuit reversed, holding that the loss of employment is an injury to business even if it resulted from a personal injury. Horn won.
